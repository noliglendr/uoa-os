# UOA OS walkthrough with Frank (30 minutes)

**Who runs it:** Noli. **With:** Frank, at his own computer, and on his phone for step 6.
**When:** before the pilot starts (Nov 30).
**Data:** the made-up Matters A, B and C only.

**How to run it:** let Frank click; you only point. Every time he stops, frowns or asks "where is…?", write it in the notes table at the end. Those are the fixes to make before the pilot.

## Before the meeting (5 minutes, Noli alone)

1. Make sure Frank can sign in and has two-step sign-in set up.
2. Put something on My work: leave one stage approval, one deadline to verify, one AI draft and one invoice draft waiting (Matter A or C).
3. Check that the morning email arrived on a business day. If not, show him the example in this file instead.

## The walkthrough

| Time | What Frank does | What to point out |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Opens **How it works** (menu, bottom) and reads the 5 steps. | This is the whole system on one page. |
| 3–10 min | On **My work**, clicks each row under "Needs your approval": approves a stage, verifies a deadline, reads and approves an AI draft, approves an invoice. | He never has to open the matter for these. Each approval is logged with his name. |
| 10–15 min | Opens **Matters**, clicks **Needs the attorney** and **Deadline in 7 days**. Opens **Matter B** (EOIR motion to reopen, urgent). | The filters answer "where am I needed?" in one click. |
| 15–20 min | On Matter B: the **Next step** card, the **Deadlines** card, the **History** tab. | The stage checklist says what is missing before a stage can move. History cannot be changed. |
| 20–24 min | Opens the **Firm dashboard**, then **Invoices**. | Fees and deadlines met, per month. |
| 24–27 min | On his **phone**: signs in, opens My work, approves one item. | Bottom tabs: My work, Matters, New, Firm, Invoices. |
| 27–30 min | Asks questions. You go through the D7 and D9 decisions that only he can make. | D7: verify the deadline rules (Firm setup → Deadline rules → Not verified). D9: when real client data may be used. |

## The morning email (example)

```
Subject: [UOA OS] 2026-11-16: 3 items need you · 1 deadline in 7 days

Good morning.

NEEDS YOU
- 1 stage approval
- 2 invoices to approve

DEADLINES IN THE NEXT 7 DAYS
- Matter A: RFE response, due in 4 days (2026-11-20)

Open My work: https://uoa-os.vercel.app/
```

It is sent on business days only, to the principal and attorneys, and only when something needs them or a deadline is within 7 days.
